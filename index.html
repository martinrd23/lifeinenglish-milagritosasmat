03 · TU PROGRAMA, SESIÓN A SESIÓN

Lo que aprenderás

Explora los dos módulos y despliega cada sesión para conocer su gramática, vocabulario, pronunciación y actividades.

Aprenderás a presentarte, hablar sobre tu familia, tus rutinas y tus gustos, y describir lugares que conoces. Toca una sesión para descubrir su contenido, pronunciación y actividades.

01Hello, I'm Milagritos!

Grammar
Subject pronouns; verb be (affirmative); contractions.

Vocabulary
Greetings, countries, nationalities and occupations.

Pronunciation
Alphabet, spelling and contractions.

Learning experience
Introduction Cards + Name Challenge; video breve; audio de presentación.

🎯 Speaking goal: Presentarte y saludar a otras personas.
02Getting to Know You

Grammar
Verb be (negative and questions); Wh- questions; possessive adjectives.

Vocabulary
Personal information, numbers and contact details.

Pronunciation
Intonation in Yes/No and Wh- questions.

Learning experience
Personal Information Detective; listening A1; mini entrevista.

🎯 Speaking goal: Hacer y responder preguntas personales.
03People in My Life

Grammar
Have/has (affirmative); possessive 's; regular plurals (-s/-es) and common irregular plurals.

Vocabulary
Family, relationships and descriptions.

Pronunciation
Plural endings: /s/, /z/ and /ɪz/.

Learning experience
Guess Who? + Family Tree Game; audio corto sobre familias.

🎯 Speaking goal: Hablar sobre tu familia y describir personas.
04My Daily Life

Grammar
Present Simple (affirmative); third-person -s; time expressions.

Vocabulary
Daily routines, telling time, days of the week.

Pronunciation
Third-person -s; sentence rhythm.

Learning experience
My Routine Timeline; escucha sobre un día habitual.

🎯 Speaking goal: Describir tu rutina y tus actividades diarias.
05Let's Talk About Our Routines

Grammar
Present Simple negatives and questions; do/does; frequency adverbs.

Vocabulary
Habits, workday activities and weekends.

Pronunciation
Do/does questions and short answers.

Learning experience
Question Wheel + Interview Challenge.

🎯 Speaking goal: Preguntar y responder sobre hábitos.
06Things I Love

Grammar
Like/love/enjoy + -ing; Present Simple review; because.

Vocabulary
Hobbies, free time, entertainment and preferences.

Pronunciation
Linking and sentence stress.

Learning experience
This or That? + canción o clip breve sobre hobbies.

🎯 Speaking goal: Expresar preferencias y dar razones sencillas.
07Places Around Me

Grammar
There is/are; prepositions of place; Where questions; plural review.

Vocabulary
Home, furniture, neighborhood and places.

Pronunciation
Location expressions and connected speech.

Learning experience
Where Is It? + picture challenge; clip de lugares cotidianos.

🎯 Speaking goal: Describir lugares y ubicar objetos.
08This Is Me!

Grammar
Integrated grammar review: sessions 1–7.

Vocabulary
Personal information, family, routines, hobbies and places.

Pronunciation
Individual pronunciation review.

Learning experience
Conversation Quest + checkpoint del primer módulo.

🎯 Speaking goal: Participar en una conversación personal guiada.

Shopping Simulation + Price Challenge.

12Can You Help Me?

14What Did You Do?

Past Simple with common irregular verbs; did/didn't; questions.

Vocabulary
Recent experiences and everyday past actions.

Pronunciation

Learning experience
Yesterday's Story + Interview Game.

🎯 Speaking goal: Preguntar y responder sobre actividades recientes.
15What's Next?

Grammar
Be going to (affirmative, negative and questions); future time expressions.

Vocabulary
Plans, appointments, weekend activities and goals.

Pronunciation
Natural rhythm in future expressions.

16Let's Have a Conversation!

Grammar
Integrated review of key structures across both modules.
